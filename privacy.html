SpendLog – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What SpendLog stores

SpendLog keeps your transactions, accounts, EMI plans and settings only on your own iPhone. There is no SpendLog server, and no data is sent to the developer.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, SpendLog uses the drive.file permission to create a "SpendLog Backups" folder and save backup files in your own Drive. It can only see files it created itself, and it cannot read any other files in your Drive. The backups are only used to restore your data. You can disconnect at any time in SpendLog's settings or in your Google account's security settings.

Email reading (optional)

If you turn on bank email alerts, SpendLog reads messages from the senders you choose directly from your mail provider, on your iPhone, to log transactions. Passwords stay in the iPhone Keychain and are never included in backups.

Sharing

SpendLog does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone.
