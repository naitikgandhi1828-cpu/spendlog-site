Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

SpendLog is a personal spending log for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what it does with it. In short: your data stays on your phone. SpendLog has no server, no user accounts and no analytics, and nothing is sent to the developer.

1. Data kept on your phone

SpendLog stores your transactions, accounts, EMI plans, categories, rules and settings in the app's private storage on your device. Passwords you type (for example a mail app password or a statement PDF password) are kept in the phone's encrypted storage and are never included in backups.

2. Google account data

When you choose Continue with Google, SpendLog asks Google for these permissions:

Read-only Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) – SpendLog searches your mailbox for emails from bank and payment senders (by default and as you choose them in the app, for example your bank's alert address), and reads those emails on your phone to create entries in your spending log: amount, currency, merchant, card or account, date, and balance or limit when the email shows it. PDF statements attached to those emails can be imported the same way. SpendLog cannot send, change, label or delete any email.

Google Drive – files created by SpendLog (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file) – SpendLog creates a "SpendLog Backups" folder in your Drive and saves backup files there, so you can restore your data on a new phone. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Your email address (email) – to show which Google account is connected and to look up the right mailbox.

Gmail messages are read directly from Google to your phone. Only the details needed for your log (listed above) are kept; the full email is not stored. Emails from senders that are not banks or payment services are not opened.

3. Limited Use of Google user data

SpendLog's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide SpendLog's features that you can see in the app (logging transactions, statements and backups).
  • It is not transferred to anyone, except to your own Google Drive when you back up, or when you yourself export or share a file from the app.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to build user profiles or train AI models.
  • No person reads your data. The developer has no access to it at all, because it never leaves your device.

4. SMS and notifications (Android, optional)

If you allow it, SpendLog reads SMS messages and notifications from banks and payment apps on your phone to log transactions the same way. They are processed on your phone only and never sent anywhere.

5. Sharing

SpendLog does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. There are no ads, no trackers and no third-party analytics.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • To stop email reading, turn it off or tap Sign out of Google in Settings → Bank email alerts.
  • To remove SpendLog's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove SpendLog.
  • Backups in your Drive stay in the "SpendLog Backups" folder until you delete them.

7. Security

All connections to Google use HTTPS. SpendLog only asks for read-only Gmail access and access to its own Drive files.

8. Children

SpendLog is not intended for children under 13.
