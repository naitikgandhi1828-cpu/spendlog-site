Using SpendLog

SpendLog is a free app for keeping a personal log of your own spending. You may use it for your own accounts and data.

No financial advice

SpendLog organises information from your bank's messages. It is not a bank, does not move money and does not give financial advice. Always check important figures with your bank.

Your data

Your data stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive backups. How the app handles data is described in the privacy policy.

No warranty

SpendLog is provided "as is". Reading bank messages automatically can miss or misread a transaction, so the developer can't guarantee the log is complete or correct and is not liable for losses from relying on it.
