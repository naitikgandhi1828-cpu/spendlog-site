How it works

  1. Open SpendLog and tap Continue with Google.
  2. Choose your Google account and allow read-only Gmail (to find bank alert emails) and SpendLog's own Drive folder (for backups).
  3. That's it. Past alerts are read straight away and new ones are logged as they arrive.

How SpendLog uses your Google data

SpendLog reads only emails from bank and payment senders (for example your bank's alert address), on your phone, to create entries in your spending log. It cannot change, send or delete any email. It creates and reads only its own backup files in your Google Drive. Your Google data is never sent to SpendLog's developer or to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold. Full details are in the privacy policy.
